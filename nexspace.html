01. Context

NexSpace is a virtual experience which brings the player through a unique experience and educates them on the topic and effects which Data Waste have on the planet.

Developed as our main project in the EPS programme at AP Hogeschool Antwerp, in a team of 8, we were brought together from a diverse set of backgrounds to develop an immersive experience and provide research alongside it for our client, AP Immersive Lab. Through our research we explored how sensors, sociology and haptic feedback can impact VR experiences.
