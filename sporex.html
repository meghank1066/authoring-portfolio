01. Context

Indoor air quality and household toxic mould (such as Stachybotrys chartarum and Aspergillus) pose significant health hazards to residents, particularly across humid European climates. Homeowners and tenants frequently identify discolouration on walls or ceilings but lack immediate tools to classify whether a strain is benign cosmetic mildew or hazardous fungal growth.

Developed as part of my MSc in Interactive Digital Media and Computer Science at Trinity College Dublin, SporeX was conceived to bridge the gap between complex biological identification and accessible mobile user interfaces.
