SPOREX — A Mould Identification Tool

Designing a clearer way to understand mould through AI-powered identification, environmental insights and user-centred guidance.

ROLE Creative Director, UX/UI Designer & Frontend Developer
TIMELINE September 2025 - May 2026
CLIENT The Asthma Society Of Ireland
TECH STACK Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Android Studio, MongoDB, Figma

Context

Mould is often easy to spot but understanding its potential health risks and knowing what to do next is far less straightforward. Renters and homeowners can struggle to interpret visible mould, environmental conditions, and conflicting advice, particularly when existing testing solutions are expensive or difficult to access.

Developed as my final-year Computing in Software Development project at DKIT, SPOREX explores how user-centred design can make complex environmental health data more accessible and actionable. I shaped the product concept, led its creative direction and designed and developed the mobile interface, translating AI-driven mould detection and environmental monitoring into clear visual feedback and practical guidance.

The Problem

The idea for SPOREX sparked when I visited a friend who had a bad case of mould in their accomodation. I approached the team with my idea and they liked it.

We set out to build a fast, cost-effective identification tool. To ensure our work targeted genuine health risks, we reached out to establish a partnership with The Asthma Society of Ireland, which shaped our understanding of how closely indoor air quality ties into respiratory health.

Recognizing that identifying mould after it already forms only solves half the battle, we also expanded our scope to develop an integrated IoT environmental sensor designed to monitor humidity, temperature and moisture levels to help high-risk users engage in active prevention.

Ultimately, renters and homeowners are left to navigate widespread indoor dampness and contradictory DIY advice without the support of explicit national housing legislation or reliable, accessible testing methods.

Research

To build a truly effective solution, we conducted targeted research across key domains: mapping common mould species, analyzing existing IoT environmental sensors, evaluating removal methods and investigating the direct health impacts of spore exposure.

During the research phase, we uncovered shocking information in relation to the problem:

50% of student rental houses suffer from daily mould growth and indoor temperatures regularly falling below 18°C, while DIY home test kits provide incomplete snapshots that frequently miss hidden contamination.
18% asthma prevalence was recorded among those exposed residents with more than double the 8% rate found in the wider local community.
0 explicit laws currently exist in Ireland to directly regulate or penalize residential mould, leaving tenants to navigate severe health hazards through indirect structural repair guidelines.

Solution

To tackle both immediate diagnosis and long-term prevention, we developed SPOREX as a dual-part ecosystem: combining an AI-powered mobile application with a dedicated IoT environmental sensor.

1. Preventive IoT Environmental Sensor: Functioning similarly to a smart security monitor, the sensor stays in a room to continuously track key environmental metrics. It calculates real-time risk scores to alert users to conditions that foster growth before mould becomes visible, providing critical protection for asthma sufferers and vulnerable groups.

2. Instant AI Mould Identification: For existing visible outbreaks, users can capture a quick photo to receive an instant species diagnosis and risk breakdown. Designed particularly with renters and businesses in mind, the free analysis removes the cost and confusion of At-Home testing.

3. Complete Remediation Guidance & Community: Beyond detection, SPOREX generates personalized remedies based on the identified mould type, user profiles to manage multiple rooms, logged case reports, and a community hub for reliable peer and expert support.

Built natively in Kotlin for Android (with an iOS release planned next), the system was iteratively validated through user testing with over 50 participants testing image capture angles, AI analysis accuracy, sensor reliability and feature prioritization.

Reflection

Reflecting on SPOREX, building both a native Kotlin app and an IoT hardware system taught me how to balance complex data with clear, non-alarmist user experiences. If I were to start over, my primary design focus would be refining the visual hierarchy between real-time sensor streams and point-in-time AI scans, as well as launching an iOS companion app side-by-side from day one.

Looking ahead, the goal is to evolve SPOREX from a capstone project into a market-ready platform. We are working toward a freemium model that keeps core monitoring free while offering monthly air quality reports and predictive exposure insights. As part of Release 5, we are also collaborating with The Asthma Society of Ireland to integrate a direct "Request a Callback" feature, connecting users with healthcare support when environmental risk levels spike.
